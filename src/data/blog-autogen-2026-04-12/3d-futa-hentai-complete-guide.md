## What Is Futanari? Definition and Origin

**Futanari** (ふたなり) is a Japanese term translating roughly to "dual nature" or "two forms." In the context of adult animation, it refers to characters who have both feminine physical presentation and a penis — intersex characters in the broadest sense, though "futanari" is the specific term used in hentai culture.

The futanari archetype in hentai is distinct from Western concepts of transgender or intersex representation. In most hentai and **3D futa** productions, futanari characters are presented as cisgender women in all aesthetic respects (feminine body, feminine presentation, female pronouns and characterization) who additionally have a penis. The character design philosophy emphasizes the femininity while adding the masculine element as an additive feature rather than a defining identity marker.
